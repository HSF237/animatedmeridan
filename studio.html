01 — Story

Fifteen years of drawing with the sun.

We are architects, engineers and craftspeople who share one obsession: buildings that feel inevitable. From hillside villas to public libraries, every project starts on paper, with a sun path, a section and a single line.

Our work is quiet in form and generous in light. We choose honest materials, stone, timber, glass and bronze, and detail them until they disappear into the experience of the place.

03 — Principles

Three ideas we never bend.

01

Light first

We model the sun before we model the walls. Every opening, overhang and material is placed to catch, soften or frame daylight.

02

Honest structure

Structure is the first layer of beauty. We expose it, refine it and let it carry the design instead of hiding behind it.

03

Materials with memory

Stone, timber, bronze and glass that age well and get better with use. We build for the fiftieth year, not the opening day.

04 — Milestones

The line so far.

2009

2013

Lisbon studio opens, and our first cultural building, Aurora Pavilion, begins.

2017
