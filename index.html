01 — The Studio

A practice built on the meridian — the line where the sun stands highest.

For over fifteen years we have designed towers, homes, cultural spaces and public landscapes across three continents. Every project begins with a single question: how will light move through this place?

Our multidisciplinary team of architects, engineers and craftspeople combines parametric design with an obsession for material, proportion and detail.

04 — Process

How a building comes alive.

Listen

We study the site, the climate, the community and your ambitions before drawing a single line.

Imagine

Rapid sketches and parametric studies explore hundreds of forms, tested against sun and wind.

Refine

Immersive 3D walkthroughs and physical models let you inhabit the design long before it's built.

Build

We stay on site through construction, protecting every detail from drawing to handover.
